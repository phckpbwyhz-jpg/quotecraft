How to price holiday-light installation without underbidding

Seasonal lighting is more than the install day. Your price needs to cover paid crew time, loading and travel, lights and clips, ladders or lifts, in-season service calls, takedown, storage and business overhead.

The calculator includes both installation and removal labor so you do not collect an attractive install price in November and then give away January labor for free.

Planning tool only. Increase time and equipment assumptions for steep roofs, multiple stories, trees, columns, complex electrical routing, custom designs, difficult access, commercial properties or lift requirements. Follow local safety, electrical and licensing rules.

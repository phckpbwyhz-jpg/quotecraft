How to price fall cleanup without underbidding

Leaf removal gets expensive when crews spend extra time blowing beds, tarping or bagging leaves, hauling debris, making dump runs and revisiting properties while trees are still dropping. Price from total paid crew time and disposal cost first, then protect the margin you need.

A seasonal package can be useful when a property needs multiple cleanups. The calculator shows both a per-visit price floor and the total seasonal price using the number of visits you enter.

Planning tool only. Adjust the time estimate for tree coverage, wet or compacted leaves, hills, fenced access, curb pickup rules, bagging, hauling distance, final mowing and any local disposal requirements.

How the calculation works

The calculator adds labor, materials, travel and allocated overhead to estimate total job cost. It then divides cost by 1 − target margin. That is different from simply marking cost up by the same percentage.

Example: a $100 job cost with a 30% target margin requires about $142.86 in selling price, not $130.

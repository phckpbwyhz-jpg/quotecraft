Why use two quote floors?

The margin formula checks whether the full estimated cost of the visit leaves enough gross profit. The labor-share check is a second guardrail for labor-heavy recurring accounts. If loaded labor alone consumes too much of the selling price, supplies, insurance, admin, callbacks and profit can get squeezed quickly.

Your target labor percentage is a management assumption, not a universal industry rule. Use your own history when you have it. The calculator simply uses the stricter of the two quote floors.

Recurring commercial cleaning

For weekly service, the calculator uses 4.333 weeks per month so a recurring monthly estimate reflects the average calendar month rather than multiplying by four. Compare the estimate with actual labor hours after the first several visits and reprice future work when the original production estimate is wrong.

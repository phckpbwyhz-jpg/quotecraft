Why this works for remote quotes

Cleaning companies often lose time driving to homes just to estimate a job. A remote pre-quote works best when the customer can provide objective details such as square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, service level, frequency and add-ons. The calculator keeps those inputs separate from your internal labor and margin check so you can give a fast estimate without exposing your cost structure.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this instead of visiting every home?

For standard jobs, it can be used as a remote pre-quote when the customer provides accurate property details. For heavily soiled, cluttered or unusual properties, confirm conditions before committing to a final price.

Should I price by square foot or by rooms?

Many operators use a hybrid rule because square footage captures overall size while bedrooms, bathrooms and add-ons capture workload that size alone misses. The calculator lets you adjust all of those assumptions.

Why is the final quote sometimes higher than my pricing-rule subtotal?

The profit check compares your customer-facing pricing rules with the cost-based minimum required to hit your selected gross margin. QuoteCraft uses the higher number so a fast remote estimate does not accidentally underprice the labor.
